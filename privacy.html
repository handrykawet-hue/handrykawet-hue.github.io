Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NARA (“NARA”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you visit this website or connect an account to a NARA service such as NARA Social Hub. NARA is a technology brand and project focused on AI, automation, and business systems.

1. Information we may process

  • basic account information supplied by a third-party platform after you authorize access, such as account ID, username, display name, or profile image;
  • authorization credentials or access tokens required to maintain an authorized connection;
  • technical information needed for security, diagnostics, and operation of the service;
  • information you voluntarily send to us by email or through a support request.

2. TikTok data

At the current integration stage, NARA Social Hub is designed to request only the minimum TikTok permission needed to identify and verify the connected account, such as user.info.basic. NARA does not enable TikTok publishing, direct messaging, or other write permissions unless those capabilities are separately added, clearly disclosed, and authorized by the user.

3. How we use information

  • to authenticate and verify the account you choose to connect;
  • to provide the requested NARA feature or integration;
  • to protect the service, detect configuration errors, and prevent unauthorized use;
  • to comply with legal obligations and platform requirements;
  • to respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests.

4. Tokens and credentials

Where supported, NARA stores authorization tokens using operating-system-level protection or another appropriate encrypted storage method. We do not intentionally publish access tokens, client secrets, or passwords in public logs or public web pages.

5. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers only where necessary to operate the service, comply with law, or support an integration you requested. Third-party platforms process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention

We retain connected-account metadata and active authorization credentials only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the integration, maintain security, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request disconnection or deletion at any time using the process described on our Data Deletion page.

7. Your choices and rights

You may revoke a platform authorization from the relevant platform account settings where available. You may also contact us to request access, correction, disconnection, or deletion of information associated with your NARA integration.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the service. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own platform accounts and credentials.

9. Children

NARA services are not directed to children. We do not knowingly design the Social Hub integration to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or platform integrations evolve. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
