Data Deletion

Last updated: 26 September 2026

You may request deletion of information associated with a NARA integration at any time.

How to request deletion

Email nara.ai.systems@gmail.com with the subject NARA Data Deletion Request. Include the platform name and the username or account identifier you want us to disconnect.

Never send us your password, client secret, access token, backup code, or other authentication secret.

What happens next

  • we verify that the request relates to the account or integration concerned;
  • we disconnect or remove active credentials under NARA's control where applicable;
  • we remove linked-account metadata that is no longer required for legitimate security, legal, or operational reasons;
  • we may ask for limited additional information if necessary to safely match the request to the correct account.

Revoke access directly

You may also revoke NARA access directly from the connected platform’s own account or security settings where that option is available.
